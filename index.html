About

Great systems make great schools.

For over four years I've worked where school operations meet technology: gathering requirements from administrators, implementing ERP modules, training teachers and principals, and keeping the infrastructure behind it all secure and running. The through-line never changed — understand how the school actually works, then make the system fit.

  • 01

    Understand the school first

    I start with the real requirements — student, staff, exam, fee and payroll structures — so the system fits how the school actually runs.

  • 02

    Train people, not just systems

    Teachers, principals and admin staff get hands-on ERP training and ongoing support, so new features get used, not just installed.

  • 03

    Keep the data accurate and secure

    I monitor databases for accuracy, integrity and security, and run backups, so schools can trust what the system tells them.

  • 04

    Deliver on time, within budget

    I manage scope, resources and risk across development, implementation and support teams — and document it all: process flows, user manuals, technical specifications.

Product

KaryaSetu. One HR system for every school and office.

HRMS for schools & offices

My HRMS for schools and offices, built for multi-campus school groups. Staff records, attendance, leave, payroll, recruitment and performance run from Head Office and are used on every campus. Each school manages its own staff; Head Office sees the whole group, approves what matters and runs payroll once for everyone.

Download brochure PDF, 2 MB

Employee management

Employee master with bulk import, staff IDs with two-level approval, QR-verified ID cards and a live org chart.

Attendance

Direct sync from eSSL and ZKTeco biometric devices, location-checked web punch, shifts with grace time and correction requests.

Leave & on duty

Requests move up the reporting line automatically. Balances, half days and holidays update attendance with no manual marking.

Payroll

CTC-based salary structures, PF, ESIC, PT and TDS, four-level approval and lock, bank transfer file and PDF payslips.

Recruitment with AI

Requisitions, vacancies and candidates per campus. AI reads resumes and ranks every applicant against the vacancy.

Performance

KRA and KPI goals per designation, monthly check-ins, principal and HR reviews, and a 1–5 rating that guides increments.

Employee self-service

Every staff member gets My Space: punch in, apply for leave, download payslips, raise requests and resign online.

Certificates & circulars

45 certificate and letter types on the school letterhead, QR-verifiable, plus numbered circulars staff acknowledge.

Also included: travel claims, assets, helpdesk, training, compliance, surveys, reports and a complete audit log.
